Post Center – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who uses this app

Post Center is a private tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own channels and pages. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly, used only to upload the owner's videos and to confirm which channel is signed in, so videos never go to the wrong channel.
  • Facebook Pages (Meta): page permissions used only to publish the owner's videos to the owner's pages.
  • TikTok: used only to send the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok inbox.

How data is stored and shared

Access tokens and video information are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platforms' own official APIs, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions, Facebook settings (Business integrations) or TikTok settings (Manage app permissions). Deleting the app's local files removes all stored data.
